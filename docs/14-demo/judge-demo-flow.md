# Judge Demo Flow

## 5–7 Minute Demonstration Flow

### Minute 0:00 – 0:45 | The Problem
- "Infrasound — pressure waves below human hearing — are generated by volcanic eruptions, severe storms, meteors, and explosions"
- "These waves travel hundreds of kilometres but are extremely difficult to detect"
- "Existing systems cost thousands of dollars and require specialized infrastructure"
- **Visual:** Problem statement slide or card

### Minute 0:45 – 1:30 | Why It's Difficult
- "Normal microphones can't detect signals below 20 Hz"
- "Wind creates noise that's bigger than the signal we want to measure"
- "Atmospheric pressure drift is millions of times larger than infrasound"
- **Visual:** Diagram comparing audible sound vs. infrasound

### Minute 1:30 – 2:30 | Our Solution
- "We built Infrasense — a low-cost infrasound monitoring system with AI"
- "Spatial-averaging manifold reduces wind noise"
- "Differential pressure with reference chamber suppresses drift"
- **Visual:** Hardware prototype, architecture diagram

### Minute 2:30 – 3:30 | Signal Acquisition and Processing
- "The sensor converts pressure to an electrical signal"
- "We digitize at 50 Hz with a high-resolution ADC"
- "Digital filtering isolates the 0.01–20 Hz range"
- "FFT reveals the frequency content"
- **Visual:** Dashboard showing live waveform and spectrum

### Minute 3:30 – 4:30 | AI Anomaly Detection
- "We trained an Isolation Forest model on normal atmospheric data"
- "The AI learns what 'normal' looks like and flags anything unusual"
- "It does NOT classify events — it detects anomalies"
- **Visual:** AI pipeline diagram

### Minute 4:30 – 5:30 | Live Demo
- "Watch the dashboard — the system is running live right now"
- "Notice the low anomaly score — everything is normal"
- *[Apply controlled test signal]*
- "Now watch — the waveform changes, FFT shows the test frequency, and..."
- "ANOMALY DETECTED — the AI flags the unusual signal"
- **Visual:** Dashboard with live anomaly detection

### Minute 5:30 – 6:15 | Applications and Impact
- "This can support atmospheric research, volcanic monitoring, educational use"
- "Low cost makes infrasound monitoring accessible to students and researchers"
- "The modular design allows continuous improvement"
- **Visual:** Use case summary

### Minute 6:15 – 7:00 | Future Scope
- "Next steps: event classification with labeled data, multi-sensor arrays for source localization, deep learning on spectrograms"
- "This prototype demonstrates that accessible infrasound monitoring with AI is feasible"
- **Visual:** Roadmap diagram

## Backup Plan

If hardware fails during the live demo:
1. Switch to pre-recorded demo data
2. Show screenshots of successful test runs
3. Walk through the architecture and explain expected behaviour
